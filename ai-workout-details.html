Overview

The AI Workout App is a next-generation fitness application designed to act as your personal trainer. By leveraging Artificial Intelligence, the app creates highly personalized workout routines tailored to your specific goals and fitness levels. It also includes an interactive AI chat assistant to answer your fitness-related queries in real-time.

Key Features

  • AI Plan Generator: Instantly generates customized daily or weekly workout routines based on user input.
  • Interactive AI Chat: A built-in smart assistant to provide instant advice on form, diet, and exercises.
  • Progress Dashboard: Beautiful visual charts and trackers to monitor your daily streaks, calories burned, and overall fitness journey.
  • Guided Exercises & Timers: Step-by-step workout screens including interactive rest timers (e.g., "rest after one step").
